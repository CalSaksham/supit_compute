A FEW THINGS, MADE CLEAR.

Ambitious vision.
Grounded approach.

Who is SUPIT for?+

AI teams renting GPU capacity who want help managing the supplier relationship, commercial terms, performance, and recovery plan. The initial focus is a narrow buyer pilot, before expanding the model.

Does SUPIT own the GPUs?+

The vision is to be the buyer’s compute management layer, working across GPU suppliers. Whether SUPIT coordinates a purchase or resells capacity is a business-model decision to validate with design partners.

How do the financial protections work?+

The concept is to negotiate holdbacks, performance credits, and recovery responsibilities directly into each agreement. The platform illustrates those economics. It does not currently hold money, issue insurance, or execute payments.

What is available today?+

A compute-proof prototype and this interactive preview of the broader platform. Financial workflows and the supply network are the product vision. The next step is a focused design-partner pilot with real supplier terms.
